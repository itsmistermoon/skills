## Instagram posts — visual structure

### Promotion / stamp card post
```
Background: #FFFFFF or #F5F4F2
- Dragon mark: top-right corner, ~120px
- Product visual: center-left, 55% of the area
- Text block (background #E81036, border-radius 16px):
    - Headline: League Spartan 800, white, uppercase
    - Subtitle: Archivo regular, white 80%
- Store name: "Imaquinaria" in Archivo semi-expanded, #5A5258, footer
```

### Level / customer congratulation post
```
Background: #211A23 (black)
- Large dragon as a watermark, opacity 10%, red
- Level badge: rounded rectangle #4D2559 (premium) or neutral
- Level name: Archivo semi-expanded, white, large (32pt+)
- "El Sendero del Bushidō": Noto Serif JP, #9E9399, small
- Logo: white, centered footer
```

### Product / news story
```
Background: #211A23 or product image
- Top bar: 6px, #E81036
- Bottom bar: 6px, #E81036
- Text inside the safe zone (140px margin at top and bottom)
- CTA sticker: background #E81036, white text, border-radius 99px
```

## Avatar and profile picture

- **Always use:** the dragon mark alone (`assets/dragon-icon.png`)
- **Never:** the full logo (wordmark + dragon) as an avatar — the text becomes illegible
- Avatar background: white `#FFFFFF` or transparent
- Do not add a frame or filters to the mark

## Typography in posts (when editing in external tools)

| Use | Font available in Canva/Figma | Weight |
|-----|-------------------------------|--------|
| Post headline | League Spartan | ExtraBold 800 |
| Subtitle / body | Archivo | Regular 400 |
| Cultural accent | Noto Serif JP | 500 |

If League Spartan is not available: **Bebas Neue** as a last resort (posts only, never official brand materials).

## GIFs and brand animations

Principles:
- Maximum duration: 3-6 seconds for loops
- No complex animated text — mobile legibility first
- The dragon may rotate or pulse gently — without distortion
- Palette: exclusively the brand colors defined above
- Frame rate: 24fps for smoothness

## Visual tone on social media

- **Dynamic but clean.** Plenty of white space. Avoid crowding the frame.
- **Products with context.** Product photos are shown in an environment (hands, desk, shelf) — not on a flat white background unless intentional.
- **The dragon has presence.** Do not hide it — it is the central symbol of the brand.
- **Background consistency.** Alternate between white `#FFFFFF`/`#F5F4F2` and black `#211A23` backgrounds for variety, without mixing them within the same post.
